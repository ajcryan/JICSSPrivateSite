Support JICSS

Your donation supports our mission to strengthen Japan's cybersecurity through research, policy advocacy, and international cooperation. Together, we can build a safer digital future.

Why Your Support Matters

JICSS brings together private sector and academic expertise to encourage practical cybersecurity approaches, build consensus, and enable protection of Japan's digital homeland through cooperation with allies and like-minded countries.

How Your Donation Will Be Used

Purpose of Donations: All donations support JICSS activities including cybersecurity research, policy advocacy, publications, seminars, and international cooperation.

Non-Profit Status: JICSS is a general incorporated association under Japanese law. We do not distribute profits to members or donors.

No Refunds: Once submitted, donations cannot be refunded.
