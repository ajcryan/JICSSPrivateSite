Donation Disclaimer

By making a donation to the Japan Institute for Cyberspace Studies (JICSS), you acknowledge and agree to the following:

Purpose of Donations

All donations to JICSS will be used exclusively to support our mission and activities, including:

  • Research and investigations related to cybersecurity and cyberspace security
  • Information provision and public awareness activities on cyberspace security
  • Establishing and promoting voluntary standards for cyberspace security
  • Publishing journals and other publications on cyberspace security
  • Planning and operating research seminars and conferences
  • Policy advocacy and cooperation with government and administrative agencies
  • Collaboration and information exchange with related organisations

Non-Profit Status

JICSS is a general incorporated association (一般社団法人) organised under Japanese law. We do not distribute profits or surplus to members or donors. Any remaining assets in the event of dissolution will be donated to public interest corporations or governmental bodies as specified in our Articles of Association.

No Refunds

Once submitted, donations cannot be refunded for any reason.

Tax Status

Please consult with your tax adviser regarding the deductibility of donations to JICSS under applicable tax laws.

Contact

For questions about how your donation will be used, please contact us at info@jicss.org
